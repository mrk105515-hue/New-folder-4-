Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

At Samridhi Art Studio, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, secure, and handle your information when you visit our website, purchase paintings, or enroll in our art academy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information to provide a seamless browsing and checkout experience:

  • Identity and Contact Info: Full name, email address, billing address, physical shipping address, and phone number when you place an order, make an inquiry, or register for courses.
  • Transaction Details: Order details, purchase history, and amounts paid. Payment gateway integration logs (excluding sensitive card credentials).
  • Device and Browsing Info: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and data collected via cookies about your interactions on our pages.

Secure Transactions: All transaction processing is carried out securely via Razorpay. We do not store or see your credit/debit card numbers, expiry dates, or CVV codes on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Order Fulfillment: To pack, ship, and deliver physical paintings, bulk collections, or customized canvases.
  • Service Administration: To set up your account on our Academy Portal and send enrollment information for courses.
  • Communication: To send tracking codes, invoices, answer inquiries about commissions, or send essential updates regarding class schedules.
  • Website Optimization: To monitor website performance, usage trends, and improve navigation flow.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted service providers to run our operations:

  • Shipping Carriers: Shipping details (name, address, phone number) are shared with logistics partners (FedEx, DHL, Blue Dart) to deliver physical orders.
  • Payment Gateways: Name, billing details, and email are processed by Razorpay to ensure secure payment authorizations.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or a judicial mandate to comply with standard legal proceedings.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to analyze web traffic, store item counts in your cart drawer, and remember your display configurations (such as wall simulator preview values). You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may limit some interactive features.

5. Data Protection Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to any inaccurate info, or ask for the deletion of your personal records. For such requests, please reach out to us at support@samridhiart.com.
