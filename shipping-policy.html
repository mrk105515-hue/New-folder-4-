Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

At Samridhi Art Studio, we are dedicated to ensuring your delicate original artwork arrives safely and in perfect condition. We wrap and pack every canvas with maximum care and use trusted shipping partners to handle all shipments.

Complimentary Shipping: We are pleased to offer FREE standard shipping on all physical artworks and bulk canvases worldwide.

1. Dispatch and Processing Timelines

Since each physical artwork is prepared, inspected, varnished, and securely packaged, please expect the following preparation times:

  • In-Stock Original Paintings: Dispatched within 2 to 3 business days from receipt of order and payment.
  • Bulk Artworks: Dispatched within 4 to 7 business days, depending on the quantity and curation requirements.
  • Custom Commissions: Timelines for custom work vary from 2 to 4 weeks depending on dry time and detailing. The precise schedule will be coordinated and agreed upon during the commissioning layout phase.

2. Shipping Timelines

Once dispatched, standard delivery transit times are as follows:

  • Domestic Shipping (Within India): Typically takes 5 to 7 business days to reach most major metro areas. Remote regions may take up to 9 business days.
  • International Shipping: Typically takes 10 to 15 business days depending on destination customs clearance processes.

3. Shipping Partners & Tracking

We utilize reliable logistics providers including FedEx, DHL, and Blue Dart to fulfill orders.

Once your painting is picked up by our shipping provider, you will receive an automated email notification containing a tracking number and a tracking link to monitor your package in real-time.

4. Delivery Failures & Address Issues

It is the customer's responsibility to provide an accurate, complete, and deliverable shipping address (including postal codes, landmarks, and contact numbers).

  • If a package is returned to our studio due to an incorrect address, missing details, or repeated failed delivery attempts, the customer will be liable for the return shipping and redelivery charges.
  • Please review your checkout details carefully to ensure timely and hassle-free receipt of your art.

5. Digital Delivery (Academy Courses)

There is no physical shipping associated with our Online Art Academy courses. Upon successful payment, enrollment access and login credentials to the academy dashboard are delivered instantly to your registered email address.
