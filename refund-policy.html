Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

At Samridhi Art Studio, we take immense pride in delivering high-quality, hand-painted original art canvases, bulk listings, customized commissions, and educational art courses. Please read our policy regarding refunds, returns, and cancellations carefully before completing a purchase.

Important Notice: All physical paintings, bulk stock orders, and customized commissions are strictly sold under a NO RETURN, NO REFUND, and NO EXCHANGE policy.

1. Physical Paintings & Artworks

Each painting available in our store (including original canvases and bulk list variants) is a unique, one-of-a-kind hand-painted asset. Because these items are hand-crafted and fragile, we do not accept returns, refunds, or exchanges once an order has been successfully placed or shipped.

We make every effort to display the colors, textures, and details of our artworks as accurately as possible. By finalizing your purchase, you acknowledge that minor hand-crafted details and variations are a natural property of original art.

2. Transit Damage Exceptions

In the extremely rare event that your painting is severely damaged during transit, we will evaluate the case for a credit or partial replacement on a case-by-case basis. To report damage:

  • You must contact us via email at support@samridhiart.com within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Your email must include clear, high-resolution photographs of the damaged shipping box, wrapping, and the damaged canvas itself, along with your Order ID.
  • Reports submitted after 24 hours from the delivery timestamp will not be eligible for review.

3. Custom Art Commissions

Customized commissions (ordered via our customized request wizard) require close collaboration, tailored materials, and specific artist labor.

  • Cancellations: You may request cancellation and a full refund of your commission deposit only within 24 hours of submitting your request, provided work has not commenced.
  • Once the design layout has been approved or painting commences, all payments (including advance deposits and final balances) are 100% non-refundable.

4. Art Academy Courses

For all online art academy courses, syllabus enrollments, and tuition fees:

  • Once enrollment is processed and payment is successfully completed, no cancellations or refunds will be issued under any circumstances.
  • In the event that a scheduled live workshop is cancelled by the instructor, a full refund of the fee paid will be credited back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

5. Transaction Refunds

If a refund or transaction adjustment is approved by us under the damages exception, the credit will automatically be applied to your original credit card or payment method via Razorpay. This process typically takes 5 to 7 business days to reflect in your account, depending on your bank's processing cycles.
